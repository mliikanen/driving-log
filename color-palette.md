Here is a cohesive color palette designed around a modern, refined **Petroleum** theme for your driving and fuel log app.

### **Primary Theme Colors**

* **Petroleum Deep (Primary Brand / Headers)**
* **HEX:** `#0F2027`
* **RGB:** `15, 32, 39`
* **Usage:** Dark navigation bars, dominant app headers, and main brand elements.


* **Oil Slick Blue (Secondary Accent / Active Elements)**
* **HEX:** `#203A43`
* **RGB:** `32, 58, 67`
* **Usage:** Active tab highlights, primary button backgrounds, and card containers.


* **Refinery Teal (Action / Interactive Accent)**
* **HEX:** `#2C5364`
* **RGB:** `44, 83, 100`
* **Usage:** Interactive icons, toggle switches, and secondary action buttons.



---

### **Functional & Data Vis Colors**

* **Fuel Gauge Gold (Fuel / Refuel Tracking)**
* **HEX:** `#FFB703`
* **RGB:** `255, 183, 3`
* **Usage:** Highlights fuel logs, gas station locations, efficiency warnings, and fuel expense charts.


* **Road Trip Emerald (Distance / Trip Metrics)**
* **HEX:** `#06D6A0`
* **RGB:** `6, 214, 160`
* **Usage:** Logged trip distances, positive efficiency trends, and GPS tracking indicators.



---

### **Backgrounds & Neutrals**

* **Asphalt Dark (Dark Mode Background)**
* **HEX:** `#12181B`
* **RGB:** `18, 24, 27`
* **Usage:** App background in Dark Mode to ensure high contrast against bright data visualizations.


* **Cool Platinum (Light Mode Background)**
* **HEX:** `#F4F7F6`
* **RGB:** `244, 247, 246`
* **Usage:** Clean, low-glare background for Light Mode daylight visibility while driving.


* **Exhaust Fog (Subtle Borders & Dividers)**
* **HEX:** `#E0E6ED`
* **RGB:** `224, 230, 237`
* **Usage:** List dividers, card borders, and inactive tab states.
