# Test photos

The pictures the Maestro flows put on the emulator (`addMedia`) and the unit tests read. The setup flow of each manifest that needs pictures (`setup.yaml` in its directory) adds them once, so a fresh emulator is enough.

| File | Used for |
|---|---|
| `photo-landscape.png` | A generated landscape image (red, white and blue bands, a green disc): the crop, the stored versions and the `picture` and `resilience` flows (uploaded by their `setup.yaml`). |
| `photo-solid-purple.png` | A generated solid color that is not one of the vehicle color presets: the color taken from a picture (the `appearance` manifest's `setup.yaml` uploads it, with `photo-car-red.jpg`). |
| `photo-car-red.jpg`, `photo-car-blue.jpg`, `photo-car-grey.jpg`, `photo-car-white.jpg` | Real photos of cars in the street: the color taken from a picture (`photo-car-red.jpg` is chosen by the `appearance` flow `color-from-photo`; all four are read by `RealPhotoColorJvmTest`, which checks the extracted color of each). They have no location data. |
| `ocr/odo/*.jpg`, `ocr/trip/*.jpg` | Real dashboard photos for reading an odometer or trip meter from a photo (`add-odometer-ocr-capture`): six car clusters in ordinary digits and six motorcycle LCDs in seven-segment digits. What the recognizer reads in each is recorded in that change's design.md. `ocr/odo/20250831_073738.jpg` (a car cluster, ODO 71140 km) and `ocr/odo/20220911_162029.jpg` (a motorcycle LCD, ODO 5034 Km) are uploaded by the `distance` manifest's `setup.yaml` and scanned by its `scan-reading` flow; `PpOcrRealPhotosJvmTest` reads all of them. They have no location data. |
| `ocr/lcd-regions.json` | The hand-marked region of the reading on each of the six LCD photos, with the reading it shows (`add-seven-segment-ocr`): the evaluation set of a seven-segment recognizer, and what its tests read. |
| `ocr/fuel/*.jpg` | Real fuel pump/receipt display photos for reading a refueling's fuel amount from a photo (`add-fuel-amount-ocr`): nine photos, five languages (Finnish, Polish, Croatian, Slovenian, and an untested English/US hypothesis), none in English. What the recognizer reads in each — including two whose volume label misreads badly enough that nothing classifies — is recorded in that change's design.md and read by `PpOcrRealPhotosJvmTest.theFuelPumpDisplays`. `ocr/fuel/DSC_0896.jpg` (7.82 L) is also uploaded by the `distance` manifest's `setup.yaml` and scanned by its `scan-reading` flow. They have no location data. |
