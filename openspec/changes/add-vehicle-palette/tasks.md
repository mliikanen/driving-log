- [ ] 1.1 Verify the libraries before anything is built on them: add `com.materialkolor.palette:core` (the Android Palette port) and `com.materialkolor:material-color-utilities` (HCT, quantizer) to the version catalog and the commonMain dependencies at the latest stable versions (confirm the exact coordinates and versions on Maven Central first), and verify (a) they resolve and compile for `androidTarget`, `iosArm64` and `iosSimulatorArm64` with Kotlin 2.4.20 and Compose Multiplatform 1.12.0, (b) a smoke test in commonTest extracts the expected colors from a small synthetic pixel array and gives the identical palette on a second run, and derives an HCT color, and (c) the debug APK size growth is recorded; if either library fails any of these, record why in the design and use the own OKLab fallback from the design for that interface instead (the rest of the tasks stay the same)
- [ ] 1.2 Add `Rgb` (opaque, `hex` in upper case, parse) and `Palette` (four `Rgb`, the storage text `RRGGBB,RRGGBB,RRGGBB,RRGGBB` and its strict parsing) and the HSV and RGB conversions for the picker, and verify unit tests for the text form (round trip, wrong count, bad hex), the conversions (round trip over a grid of colors, primaries, greys, black and white) and `hex`
- [ ] 1.3 Add `PaletteDeriver` and `HctPaletteDeriver` (the first color is the chosen color exactly; analogous, tonal, soft and opposite candidates, then tone steps; the distance threshold; deterministic) and verify unit tests: the first color is the input for every preset, four distinct colors for every preset and for a sweep of hues and tones, for white, black and mid grey, the same input gives the same palette, the other colors are in the same family for a saturated color
- [ ] 1.4 Add `PaletteExtractor`, the selection and fill rules (candidate order, the distance threshold, lightness-variation fill through the deriver) and `KmPalettePaletteExtractor` (the central 80% of the samples, transparent pixels replaced by the average opaque color, fewer than 1% opaque gives null, no filters, at most 16 colors), and verify unit tests through the real library on synthetic pixels: solid color (first color is that color, three variations), two halves (both colors present), white with a little dark grey (white and dark grey present), red square in a blue border (red first), a photo of a red vehicle in front of a blue wall cropped to the vehicle (no wall blue), a transparent image (null), partly transparent, and the same input twice
- [ ] 1.5 Add `vehiclePalette` (a picture's palette wins; else derived from the main color; else none) and verify unit tests on every combination of picture, picture palette and main color, including a picture whose palette is null falling back to the color
